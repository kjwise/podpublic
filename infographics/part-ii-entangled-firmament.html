IV

Bounded Infinity

The Local Aperture

LORENZ ATTRACTOR Bounded region. Non-repeating trace.

The Lorenz attractor traces changing motion within a finite region. The book extends this as a contemplative possibility: actual infinity can express locally through finite form. The boundary acts as an aperture, giving one rendering contour without containing or exhausting its source.

Computational Irreducibility

Choose A Map · List · Tree · Graph

Computational irreducibility means: in some rule-driven systems, there is no general shortcut to the exact outcome. You learn where it lands by running the steps.

Scope note: this does not mean “nothing is predictable.” It means exact long-run detail can resist compression even when useful forecasts still exist.

The Two Minds Within

analytic / story-building mode

One Distributed Mind · Heuristic

  • Structure & Syntax
  • Categories & Naming
  • Sequencing in Time
  • Step-by-step Reasoning

Example: outlining an argument, debugging a sentence, following a recipe step-by-step.

contextual / holistic mode

One Distributed Mind · Heuristic

  • Context & Pattern
  • Tone & Relational Cues
  • Present-Moment Sensing
  • Implicit / Tacit Meaning

Example: sensing tension in a room, catching a subtle shift in tone, noticing what your body is signaling.

These are functional modes, not a clean left/right split. Real cognition is distributed and integrated; both modes typically operate together.

Cross-Talk

(Across a Distributed Mind)

Coordination / Information Exchange

No single pathway or hemisphere owns either mode.

Storyteller’s Throne

Storyteller’s Throne is a subordinate mythic image for narrative stitching, not an anatomical seat, a third mode, or a separate framework. Split-brain research describes an interpreter effect that can appear when information is limited; more generally, the mind can fill gaps with a continuous story from partial data.

Better cross-talk gives the analytic/story-building mode more sensory and contextual data, including interoception, so narratives remain answerable to experience. This is a heuristic about attention and information flow, not a diagnosis.
