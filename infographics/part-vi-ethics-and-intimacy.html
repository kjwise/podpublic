The Prism of Impact

Intent, Beam, Prism, Impact, Refraction

Intent is what you meant. The Beam is what you actually sent. The Prism is the whole encounter across which Impact lands and Refraction emerges. Do not use this tool to erase impact or debate someone in pain.

Reading Divergence Cleanly

Intent is your aim or account. The Beam is observable conduct. The Prism is the irreducible boundary and whole encounter; Impact is what lands, while Refraction is what the encounter comes to mean.

Legible Beam: conduct is easier to name, but intent still does not erase impact and some refraction remains.

Beam: words, tone, action, leverage. Prism: sender, receiver, injury, power, state, context. Impact: what lands. Refraction: what is inferred.

Use

For self-audit, slower reality checks, repair where real, and boundary or oversight when needed. Not for winning a narrative with projection language.

The Wheel of Consent

Who is doing? Who is it for?

Use the Wheel for touch, attention, conversation, and facilitation. The point is not speed. It is clarity, living consent, and enough pause that a clean yes or no can stay sovereign.

Living-Consent

Treat the agreement as ongoing and revocable. If the body wavers, pause. Clarity is cleaner than momentum.

Processing Time

The Wheel does not have to happen in real time. Step away, check the body, and come back with a truer answer.

The Threads of Trust

Trust is not a binary switch.

When trust feels shaky, do not ask “Do I trust them?” Ask which thread is fraying. Specific diagnosis gives you a cleaner response: repair, boundary, or a quieter exit.

S

Sincerity

"Do you mean what you say?"

Are values visible, or is the signal mostly image and spin?

R

Reliability

"Do you do what you say?"

Can the nervous system stop bracing because follow-through is consistent?

C

Competence

"Can you do what you promised?"

Good intent is not enough if the person lacks skill, scope, or capacity.

C

Care

"Do you hold my interest with yours?"

Do they keep your dignity in frame, or only their own urgency and need?

Name the Thread

“I trust your care, but not your reliability” is far more useful than “I don’t trust you.” Specificity turns judgment into discernment.

Repair With a Seam

A repair is a conscious fold in the relational fabric. You can mend what frayed without collapsing a person into identity.

The Ethical Compass

When Heat Rises

These tools are scaffolding, not trophies. Presence, consent, and truthful response are the work. Use the compass for self-audit, cleaner boundaries, and repair where real.

Reality Tether
Start with what is observable. Acknowledge their experience without swallowing it, state what you actually did, name the gap, and set the next boundary or pause.

If you hold structural power, treat feedback as signal and do not judge your own case. Name what recourse actually exists. These tools are not for dismissing someone naming harm.
