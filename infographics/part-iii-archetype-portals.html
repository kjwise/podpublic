The Shadow Threshold

Integration, Not Exorcism

The Shadow is the archive of what was split off to survive. Dark, Gray, and Golden Shadow each hold charge asking for witness, not performance.

The Work: Reclaim what was exiled, then keep it in ethical custody rather than turning it into display.

Present-Layer Contact

Parent, Child, and Sibling are the survival layer of intimacy. The Lover is the present layer that can emerge when those scripts loosen enough for the body to find some choice beyond control, collapse, or scorekeeping.

Prism of Impact

Intent is what the sender means; the Beam is observable conduct. The Prism is the irreducible boundary and whole encounter. Impact is what lands, while Refraction is the interpretation that emerges through sender, receiver, and shared context.

Grace Margin

Not every friction is Shadow. Leave room for hunger, fatigue, and ordinary clumsiness; if a pattern is unsafe, boundaries come first.
