The Dragon Incarnate

Ordinary Evidence of the Work

COHERENCE

"The Dragon is the way you stand in line at the grocery store."

Breath

Expression that stays warm and precise under pressure. The exhale clears static so the next words land clean.

Claws

Traction and definition. A clean boundary draws the line without rigidity, winning, or extra force.

Scales

Resilient permeability. Not a fortress, not a floodgate: available for contact without taking every impact inside.

SERVICE CHECK

Feeling proud of your growth is healthy. Feeling special, separate, or superior because of it is the trap. Coherence is evidence, not self-certification: keep it open to correction from body, evidence, conduct, relationship, and consequence. The bigger the Dragon, the greater the service.
