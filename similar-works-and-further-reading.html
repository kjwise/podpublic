Part X

Similar Works and Further Reading

Estimated reading time: 4 min

This shelf gathers works on archetypal inquiry, trauma, systems, ethics, ritual, and altered states. Choose by the question you are living with. A memoir, a clinical synthesis, and a work of philosophy each offer different kinds of knowledge.

Think of this bibliography as a shelf, not a syllabus. Inclusion here is not an endorsement; use it to widen your references without outrunning integration.

Depth & Archetypal Psychology

  • Carl Jung — Man and His Symbols

    An introduction to archetypes, dreams, and the symbolic psyche through everyday images and experience.

  • James Hillman — Re-Visioning Psychology

    Psychology as soul-making, with image, imagination, and a polytheistic psyche at its center.

  • Joseph Campbell — The Hero with a Thousand Faces

    An influential descent–return model to set beside the Spiral Path. Read the “monomyth” as a comparative interpretation, not a universal account of cultures.

  • Clarissa Pinkola Estés — Women Who Run With the Wolves

    Stories of instinct, intuition, boundary, and voice; a route into archetypal inquiry through myth.

Somatic & Trauma-Informed Healing

  • Bessel van der Kolk — The Body Keeps the Score

    An influential clinical synthesis of trauma’s effects on body, brain, memory, and relationship. An entry point rather than a final scientific authority.

  • Babette Rothschild — The Body Remembers

    A practical bridge between psychotherapy and somatic regulation, emphasizing pacing and choice.

  • Peter Levine — Waking the Tiger

    Introduces Levine’s approach to trauma through orienting, pendulation, and discharge.

  • Deb Dana — The Polyvagal Theory in Therapy

    Practitioner tools for co-regulation and noticing changes of state. Their usefulness does not settle the theory’s debated neurobiological claims.

  • Francis Weller — The Wild Edge of Sorrow

    A communal and ritual approach to grief, including the Five Gates of Grief used in Part IV (Practices for Embodied Transformation).

Science, Systems & the Entangled Firmament

  • Fritjof Capra — The Tao of Physics

    A comparison of physics and Eastern thought. Resonance between them does not establish equivalence.

  • David Bohm — Wholeness and the Implicate Order

    The implicate and explicate orders: a philosophical and physical inquiry into wholeness and enfoldment.

  • Stephen Wolfram — A New Kind of Science

    Simple rules and emergent complexity. Wolfram’s later Ruliad, the entangled limit of possible computations, is distinct from this book’s adapted Everfolding ℛ; neither comparison establishes settled physics.

  • Frank Close — The Void

    A physicist’s account of emptiness. Scientific vacuum and the contemplative Void remain different subjects.

  • Melanie Mitchell — Complexity: A Guided Tour

    An overview of emergence, networks, and adaptation for readers seeking firmer scientific grounding in complexity.

  • Donella Meadows — Thinking in Systems

    Leverage points, delays, and unintended consequences: practical help in deciding where and how to act.

  • Humberto Maturana & Francisco Varela — The Tree of Knowledge

    Autopoiesis and the biology of cognition, exploring how knowing arises through embodied relationship with an environment.

Myth, Ritual & Symbolic Imagination

  • Archive for Research in Archetypal Symbolism (ARAS) — The Book of Symbols (Taschen)

    A visual index for dreamwork and archetypal study. Follow its images into their historical and religious contexts before borrowing them.

  • Starhawk — The Spiral Dance

    Ritual, embodiment, and communal practice through cycles of grief, renewal, and power.

  • Georg Feuerstein — Tantra: The Path of Ecstasy

    A scholar-practitioner synthesis of Tantric philosophy and discipline. One interpretive lens, not a complete history of Hindu and Buddhist Tantric traditions.

  • David Gordon White — Kiss of the Yogini

    Historical study of Tantric ritual and transgression, including sexual rites; useful for distinguishing lineage contexts from modern enactments.

  • Hugh B. Urban — Tantra: Sex, Secrecy, Politics, and Power in the Study of Religion

    How Tantra has been eroticised, politicised, and remade, including in Western and Neo-Tantric settings.

Ethics, Power & Plural Perspectives

  • School of Consent — Wheel of Consent resources

    Study Betty Martin’s Wheel in its own terms: who acts, whom the action is for, and how clear agreements support choice. This educational route does not replace activity-specific training.

  • NCSF Consent Counts — consent education and negotiation

    A US-situated resource for consent materials and negotiated kink; its legal material is not universal law, and its educational guidance does not replace activity-specific training.

  • Julie Diamond — Power: A User’s Guide

    Rank, role, and influence, with tools for recognizing power and taking responsibility for its use.

  • Dossie Easton & Janet Hardy — The Ethical Slut

    A practical manual on consent and communication whose stated domain is polyamory, open relationships, sex, and love.

  • Thich Nhat Hanh — Interbeing: Fourteen Guidelines for Engaged Buddhism

    Buddhist precepts that bring non-separation into speech, listening, and daily conduct.

  • Resmaa Menakem — My Grandmother’s Hands

    A somatic approach to racialised trauma and repair across communities.

  • Bayo Akomolafe — These Wilds Beyond Our Fences

    Letters questioning urgency, control, and familiar ways of understanding our relations with the world.

  • Malidoma Patrice Somé — Of Water and the Spirit

    An initiatory memoir about ritual and community. One person’s account, not a comprehensive representation of Dagara knowledge or a portable ritual manual.

Neurodiversity & Altered States

  • Steve Silberman — NeuroTribes

    A cultural history of autism that foregrounds inclusion and the design of environments.

  • Devon Price — Unmasking Autism

    Guidance for recognizing camouflage and reclaiming choice in expression, relationships, and daily life.

  • Stanislav Grof & Hal Zina Bennett — The Holotropic Mind

    A transpersonal map of psyche and non-ordinary states, offering comparative language rather than clinical screening guidance.

  • Michael Pollan — How to Change Your Mind

    A journalistic introduction to psychedelic history and research, not individualized medical guidance or a practice protocol.

Take what meets the work in front of you, and let integration set the pace.
